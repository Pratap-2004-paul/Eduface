About Us

Built for Indian educational institutions

SmartAttend was built to solve the daily pain of manual attendance — messy registers, proxy attendance, and hours of data entry every week.

Our system uses state-of-the-art face recognition AI to make the process instant, accurate, and completely digital. From small coaching centers to large colleges, SmartAttend scales to your needs.
